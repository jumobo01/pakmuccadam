Governing Documents

Association Documents

The founding and governing documents of the Pakistan Muccadam Association, available for members and stakeholders to review and download.

MoA

Memorandum of Association

The foundational document setting out the Association’s name, registered office, objects, powers, and governing conditions under Section 42 of the Companies Act, 2017.

AoA

Articles of Association

Governs the internal rules of the Association — membership eligibility, admission, rights and duties of members, the Governing Body, and meeting procedures.

PDF Document
Download PDF
Chr

Charter of the Association

Sets out PMA’s working structure and scope of functions — representation on Muccadam charges, legal support, advisory services, and professional standards.

JRB

Joint Representation of Muccadam Companies to Banks

A formal representation submitted on behalf of all participating Muccadam Companies, urging banks to review Muccadam charges and address key operational challenges. Includes Annexure A, a bank-wise schedule of current Muccadam charges.
