General Secretary

Ameer Abdullah Khan Niazi

Ameer Abdullah Khan Niazi

Chief Executive Director | Banking & Muccadam Services Professional

Professional Profile

Mr. Ameer Abdullah Khan Niazi is a highly accomplished banking and corporate professional with more than thirty (30) years of extensive experience in the fields of banking, Muccadam services, collateral management, stock verification, valuation, hypothecation inspections, and financial consultancy. Throughout his distinguished career, he has earned a reputation for professionalism, integrity, and excellence in providing specialized services to banks, financial institutions, and corporate organizations across Pakistan.

Professional Journey

Mr. Niazi commenced his professional career with Askari Bank in Faisalabad, where he served for nearly five years and gained valuable experience in banking operations, credit administration, and financial services. In 1999, he joined Harvester Services (Pvt.) Ltd. as General Manager. During his approximately ten-year tenure, he played a vital role in expanding the company’s operational capabilities, strengthening client relationships, and enhancing service quality for financial institutions throughout Pakistan.

Subsequently, he joined National Cargo (Pvt.) Ltd. as Executive Director, where he further broadened his expertise in corporate management and banking support services. He later assumed the position of Chief Executive Director of Hunza Services (Pvt.) Ltd., where he currently serves. Under his leadership, the company has grown into one of Pakistan’s well-recognized Muccadam service providers, delivering a comprehensive range of professional services to major commercial banks and financial institutions with an operational network extending across the country.

Areas of Expertise

  • Muccadam (Warehousing) Services
  • Stock Verification and Monitoring
  • Hypothecation Inspections
  • Collateral Management
  • Asset Valuation and Evaluation
  • Banking Consultancy
  • Credit Risk Support Services
  • Corporate Management
  • Banking Compliance
  • Financial Institution Coordination

Professional Contributions

Over the course of his career, Mr. Niazi has successfully provided specialized services to numerous commercial banks, development finance institutions, and corporate organizations throughout Pakistan. His practical knowledge of banking operations, collateral management, and risk mitigation has enabled financial institutions to safeguard their lending portfolios through accurate inspections, reliable reporting, and professional consultancy services. In addition to his operational leadership, he has served as a trusted consultant for various banks and corporate firms, advising on matters relating to stock verification, collateral assessment, operational procedures, and banking support services.

Leadership Philosophy

Mr. Ameer Abdullah Khan Niazi firmly believes that integrity, accountability, professionalism, and continuous improvement are the cornerstones of sustainable business success. Through his extensive experience and visionary leadership, he continues to contribute significantly to Pakistan’s banking support industry while promoting excellence in Muccadam services, collateral management, and financial consultancy across the country.

Pakistan Muccadam Association

A professional, non-profit, non-political representative body of Muccadam companies operating across Pakistan — formed to promote integrity, trust, and fair representation between Muccadam companies and the banking sector.
