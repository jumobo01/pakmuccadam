Eligibility

Who Can Become a Member

Eligible Entity

A sole proprietorship, partnership firm, association of persons, or company holding a national tax number and sales tax registration (if applicable) in the name of the business.

Enlisted Muccadam Company

Enlisted by one or more banks and with the Pakistan Banks’ Association to handle pledge sites, representing banks as their agents, supervising stock, and providing periodic stock reports.

Proposed & Seconded

Applications for membership must be proposed and seconded by existing Members of the Association.

Good Standing

The prospective member must have no criminal conviction and must agree to abide by the Association’s Articles, rules, and code of conduct.

For membership enquiries, please contact the Association.

Pakistan Muccadam Association

A professional, non-profit, non-political representative body of Muccadam companies operating across Pakistan — formed to promote integrity, trust, and fair representation between Muccadam companies and the banking sector.
