Working Structure & Scope of Functions

Scope of the Association

PMA works collectively with all member Muccadam companies across six core functions — each aimed at strengthening the industry while maintaining a cooperative relationship with banks.

01

Representation on Muccadam Charges

Advocating for fair, realistic, and economically justified revision of Muccadam charges, and presenting collective representations to banks based on inflation, operational costs, risk exposure, and compliance obligations — as a unified professional voice rather than individual representations.

02

Legal Support & Protection for Members

Providing support and assistance to member companies on disputes with banks, matters related to pledged stock, custodial responsibilities and liabilities, and legal notices, inquiries, or proceedings — along with coordinated legal strategies where appropriate.

03

Professional & Legal Support to Banks

Operating on cooperation rather than confrontation: providing advisory support to banks on pledge management, stock supervision, and legal frameworks, and helping clarify responsibilities to reduce disputes.

04

Advisory Support to Member Companies

Advising and guiding member companies facing operational challenges or ambiguity in banking instructions or contractual terms, with services aimed at preventive risk management and dispute avoidance.

05

Professional Standards & Ethics

Promoting and upholding professional conduct, integrity, transparency, and accountability among members, with the objective of maintaining consistent and reliable Muccadam services across Pakistan.

06

Policy Engagement & Stakeholder Coordination

Engaging with banks and other stakeholders at a policy level, advocating for frameworks that ensure sustainability for Muccadam companies while safeguarding banks’ interests.

From the Memorandum of Association

Core Objectives

Rights, Welfare & Coordination

To promote and protect the rights, interests, and welfare of Muccadams throughout Pakistan, and to establish coordination and unity among members.

Representation Before Authorities

To represent members before government departments, public authorities, and other organisations.

Professional Development

To organise meetings, seminars, workshops, and training programs for the professional development of members.

Legal Guidance

To provide legal guidance and support to members where permissible under the law, applicable in the daily life of the industry.

See the full Memorandum & Articles of Association for the complete list of objects and powers of the Association.

Pakistan Muccadam Association

A professional, non-profit, non-political representative body of Muccadam companies operating across Pakistan — formed to promote integrity, trust, and fair representation between Muccadam companies and the banking sector.
