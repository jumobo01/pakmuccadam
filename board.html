Structure

Executive Members

All other member Muccadam companies are represented through their respective owners, CEOs, or proprietors, who collectively form the Executive Members of the Association. The Governing Body exercises full authority over the policy direction, administration, and representation of the Association, and is empowered to take all decisions necessary to fulfil PMA’s objectives.

Pakistan Muccadam Association

A professional, non-profit, non-political representative body of Muccadam companies operating across Pakistan — formed to promote integrity, trust, and fair representation between Muccadam companies and the banking sector.
