22nd June 2026

Muccadam Company Owners Meet to Endorse Formation of PMA

A meeting of Muccadam company owners from across Pakistan was held on 22nd June 2026 at 2:00 PM at Rose Palace Hotel. Owners representing Muccadam companies attended the gathering, where the formation of the Pakistan Muccadam Association was discussed and unanimously agreed upon. Attendees extended their full support to the Managing Board of the Association, marking a significant step toward a unified, professional platform for the Muccadam industry in Pakistan.

Pakistan Muccadam Association Formally Established

The Pakistan Muccadam Association (PMA) has been formed as a professional, non-profit, and non-political representative body of Muccadam companies operating across Pakistan. The Association brings together Muccadam companies under a unified, principled platform to engage constructively with banks and regulators on matters of shared interest.

Read the full background in About the Association, or view the founding documents on the Documents page.
