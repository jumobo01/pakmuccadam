Vice Chairman

Ashfaq Awan

Ashfaq Awan

Vice Chairman & Chief Executive Officer-Awan Engineers & Surveyors (Pvt) Ltd

Professional Profile

Mr. Ashfaq Awan is a seasoned business leader, legal professional, and corporate administrator with more than fourteen (14) years of diversified experience in legal practice, corporate governance, banking compliance, and business management. A graduate of the University of the Punjab, he has established himself as a highly competent professional with an exceptional understanding of regulatory frameworks, financial institutions, and corporate administration.

He is also responsible for coordinating and managing correspondence with commercial banks, development finance institutions, and regulatory authorities. His strong relationships within Pakistan’s banking sector have significantly contributed to strengthening institutional confidence and ensuring the seamless execution of banking-related assignments.

Leadership & Corporate Management

Mr. Ashfaq Awan is the Founder, Owner, and Chief Executive Officer of M/s Awan Engineers & Surveyors (Pvt.) Ltd., one of Pakistan's leading professional service organizations providing integrated solutions to banks, financial institutions, corporate entities, and government organizations throughout the country.

Under his visionary leadership, the company has developed into a trusted nationwide organization offering comprehensive professional services under one roof while maintaining the highest standards of transparency, technical competence, and regulatory compliance.

Vice Chairman – Pakistan Muccadam Association (PMA)

In addition to his corporate responsibilities, Mr. Awan proudly serves as the Vice Chairman of the Pakistan Muccadam Association (PMA), the representative body working for the promotion, professional development, and standardization of Muccadam (Warehousing) services across Pakistan.

Through his leadership within PMA, he actively contributes to strengthening professional standards, promoting ethical business practices, enhancing collaboration between the banking sector and warehousing professionals, and advocating policies that improve the efficiency, transparency, and credibility of collateral management and stock monitoring services nationwide.

Core Professional Services

  • Warehousing (Muccadam Services)
  • Stock Inspection and Verification
  • Search Reports
  • Registration of Charges with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP)
  • Supervision of Customs Clearance of Imported Goods under the Finance Against Imported Merchandise (FIM) facility
  • Valuation and Assessment Services
  • Property Benchmarking
  • Corporate Compliance and Banking Support Services

Professional Excellence

Throughout his career, Mr. Awan has built a reputation for integrity, transparency, and technical excellence. His leadership has enabled the organization to become a preferred service provider for banks, financial institutions, leasing companies, modarabas, corporate organizations, and public-sector entities. His expertise in banking operations, documentation, regulatory compliance, and operational risk management has consistently helped clients safeguard their financial interests while maintaining compliance with regulatory requirements.

Government & Institutional Assignments

Under Mr. Awan’s leadership, the company has successfully executed numerous assignments for public and private sector organizations. A notable achievement includes providing professional assessment, valuation, and benchmarking services to Railway Estate Development and Marketing Company (REDAMCO) (Pvt.) Ltd, an organization operating under the Ministry of Railways, Government of Pakistan.

Banking & Financial Sector Expertise

  • Corporate Governance
  • Banking Documentation
  • Credit Administration Support
  • Regulatory Compliance
  • Collateral Verification
  • Stock Monitoring
  • Banking Correspondence
  • Risk Mitigation
  • Corporate Administration
  • Institutional Coordination

Leadership Philosophy

Mr. Ashfaq Awan believes that professionalism, integrity, accountability, and continuous improvement form the foundation of sustainable organizational success. Through visionary leadership and a commitment to excellence, he continues to strengthen the firm’s reputation as a trusted professional partner for banking, corporate, and government institutions throughout Pakistan.

Pakistan Muccadam Association

A professional, non-profit, non-political representative body of Muccadam companies operating across Pakistan — formed to promote integrity, trust, and fair representation between Muccadam companies and the banking sector.
